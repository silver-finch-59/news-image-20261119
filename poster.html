THURSDAY, 19 NOVEMBER 2026
Five stories that defined the day — from Shenzhen to The Hague.
DIPLOMACY — LEAD STORY

Three Leaders, One Table

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen on Thursday — the first-ever trilateral of the leaders of the world’s three largest nuclear powers, on the sidelines of the APEC summit. Xi hugged Trump on the tarmac as he arrived; nuclear arms control and the war in Ukraine top the agenda.

Analysts expect a striking photograph, not a breakthrough: the three could not agree on a single shared agenda.

02UKRAINE

Crimean Bridge Hit Again

Traffic is suspended over the Crimean Bridge after a third attack in a week, Russia’s Crimea governor says, hours before Putin’s Shenzhen meetings. Earlier this week Ukrainian strikes destroyed the bridge’s central road span.

03JUSTICE

World Court Rules on Myanmar

The International Court of Justice delivers its judgment today in the Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar — the first time an international court could find a state guilty of genocide. The Gambia brought the case in 2019 over the 2017 crackdown.

04MARKETS

Anthropic’s $81bn Record IPO

Claude-maker Anthropic prices the biggest IPO ever at $83 a share, raising $81.3 billion and valuing the AI lab at about $1.5 trillion. Shares debut on the Nasdaq today under the ticker ANT; Asian stocks rise on the news.

05US POLITICS

Jeffries Nominated for Speaker

House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries 251–11 to lead the next Congress, putting him on course to become the first Black Speaker in US history. The full House votes on 3 January, when the new Congress convenes.

ALSO TODAY

Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4m

A Van Gogh painting fetches a record $190.4 million at auction, after a row over its Nazi-era history.
